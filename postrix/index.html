Postrix

External security posture scanner. Try it below. It checks the public website you enter: its security headers, its redirects and its DMARC email record. Only check sites you own or are allowed to test.

Problem
Missing security headers, weak redirects and no DMARC record are the first things attackers check on a public site, and assessments often live in one-off spreadsheets.
Built
A posture platform that runs passive checks of a public website (security headers, redirects and DMARC), tracks vulnerabilities from discovery to fix, and produces remediation blueprints and reports, with workspaces per client and role-based access.
Gives you
A posture score and header grade for each site, with the fixes written out and ready to hand over.
