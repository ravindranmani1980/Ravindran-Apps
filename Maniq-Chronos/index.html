Maniq Chronos

Team project and task workspace that works offline. Try it below: it runs in your browser with built-in sample data.

Problem
Small teams track tasks, deadlines and who can change what across chats, spreadsheets and calendars, and lose track when they are offline.
Built
A project and task workspace with board and table views, calendar scheduling with Google Calendar links, priority labels, role-based access for team members, deadline alerts and productivity analytics. It works offline and installs as an app.
Gives you
Sample projects to explore straight away, and Google sign-in to save your own projects and sync them across devices.
