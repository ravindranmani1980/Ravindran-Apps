Maniq Govern

Architecture, governance and adoption analytics for Microsoft Copilot. Try it below: it runs in your browser with built-in sample data.

Problem
Rolling out Microsoft Copilot across personal and enterprise accounts raises questions about sign-in, security policy and whether people actually use it.
Built
A step-by-step studio that explains Copilot features, models a unified multi-tenant sign-in for individuals and team administrators, and adds adoption and usage analytics, security and compliance review for Azure AI services, and log export to cloud storage.
Gives you
Realistic sample adoption reports and compliance reviews for stakeholders, with the option to connect a live tenant.
